Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Joe's Motiv Painting Service.

Do you provide free estimates?
Yes, 100%! We provide free on-site or virtual estimates across Amarillo, Canyon, Pampa, Borger, Dumas, and Dalhart. You can reach out directly via WhatsApp or phone at +1 806-576-5859.
How long does a typical interior room painting project take?
A standard single bedroom or living room usually takes 1 to 2 days, including full drywall patching, taping, priming, and two topcoats. Larger multi-room or whole-home projects are scheduled with clear timelines before work starts.
Do you offer cabinet painting and refinishing?
Yes! Cabinet painting is one of our specialty services. We thoroughly degrease, sand, apply high-adhesion bonding primer, and finish with smooth, durable cabinet enamel to give you a brand-new kitchen look.
Do I have to move all my furniture before you arrive?
We ask that you remove small personal keepsakes and breakables. We help shift heavy furniture to the center of the room and seal everything under protective plastic sheeting and canvas drop cloths.
What paint brands do you use?
We primarily use premium paints from Sherwin-Williams and Behr. We can also accommodate any specific brand, color, or sheen level you prefer.
Is Joe's Motiv Painting Service licensed and insured?
Joe's Motiv Painting Service is currently working toward becoming fully licensed, operates with professional standards, and stands 100% behind the quality and integrity of every project.
