Got Questions?

Services FAQ

Common questions regarding our painting options and procedures.

Can you paint high ceilings and vaulted spaces?
Yes! We have specialized extension equipment, ladders, and scaffolding to safely paint high vaulted ceilings, grand entryways, and two-story focal walls.
How durable is cabinet painting compared to new cabinets?
When properly degreased, scuffed, primed with bonding primer, and coated with specialized urethane enamel, painted cabinets will resist chipping and moisture for 7 to 10+ years of daily use.
Do you offer exterior painting year-round?
We paint exteriors during favorable weather windows when temperatures are consistently above 50°F and winds are manageable. Spring, summer, and early fall are ideal in the Texas Panhandle.
What sheen should I choose for living rooms and bedrooms?
Eggshell or satin is the most popular choice for living areas and bedrooms because it offers an attractive soft luster with great washability. Flat/matte is great for ceilings to hide imperfections.
How do I get a quote for multiple rooms?
Simply message us on WhatsApp or fill out our online contact form. You can send photos or schedule an on-site visit where Joe will measure and provide a comprehensive itemized quote.
