Our Story & Roots

Bringing Pride & Precision To Every Panhandle Job

Joe’s Motiv Painting Service was started with a simple belief: Amarillo homeowners deserve a painting contractor who treats their home with respect, communicates honestly, and never cuts corners on preparation.

From humble beginnings painting single rooms and accent walls for friends and neighbors, we have steadily grown by earning repeat referrals and 5-star reviews throughout Amarillo, Canyon, Pampa, and neighboring communities.

Our business is currently working toward becoming fully licensed and continues to invest in professional equipment, premium materials, and ongoing training to serve our community at the highest level.

Owner Commitment

The "Motiv" Behind Our Work

The name "Motiv" comes from our daily motivation: leaving every customer completely thrilled with their refreshed space. We know inviting a contractor into your home requires trust.

That is why owner Joe personally oversees every project, from the initial on-site estimate to the final coat and post-project walkthrough. You never have to wonder who is showing up to work on your property.

Direct Communication Guarantee
Have a question or change in mind? Joe is just a phone call or WhatsApp message away before, during, and after your project.
Core Principles

Values That Guide Every Job

These four pillars ensure consistent quality and customer satisfaction on every job site.

1. Meticulous Prep

Proper cleaning, sanding, drywall patching, and caulking account for 80% of how long your paint will last.

2. Zero Hidden Fees

What we quote is what you pay. Transparent pricing with all materials and labor clearly detailed in advance.

3. Respect For Your Home

Drop cloths everywhere, plastic-wrapped furnishings, no smoking on premises, and clean daily wrap-ups.

4. Local Panhandle Pride

We are invested in Amarillo and surrounding towns. Your satisfaction directly impacts our local reputation.

Clean Site Policy

We Treat Your Home Like A Sanctuary

One of the biggest homeowner fears is paint splatter on hardwood floors, tile grout, or furniture. We eliminate that fear completely.

Every project begins by covering all walking areas with heavy-duty drop cloths, securing light fixtures in poly wrap, and masking baseboards with premium tape. At the end of each work session, we pack tools away and vacuum debris.
