Our Interior Workflow

How we protect your home and ensure beautiful, long-lasting results.

1
Protect
Floors covered with thick canvas cloths; furniture moved and covered with plastic wrap.
2
Repair
Drywall holes, cracks, and nail pops patched with spackle and sanded smooth.
3
Mask
Baseboards, windows, ceiling lines, and light switches masked with blue painter's tape.
4
Paint

5
Inspect
Tape removed cleanly, floors vacuumed, and homeowner walks the space for final sign-off.

Questions Answered

Interior Painting FAQ

How long do interior walls take to dry completely?
Most latex and acrylic paints are dry to the touch within 1 to 2 hours and can be recoated in 4 hours. They fully cure within 14 to 30 days. You can safely return furniture to the room the same evening.
Can you repair drywall holes before painting?
Yes, drywall patching and nail hole repair is a standard part of our surface preparation on every interior project. We sand patches smooth and prime them before painting.
Do low-VOC paints smell during painting?
No! We utilize premium low-VOC and zero-VOC paints that have minimal odor during application and dissipate almost immediately, making them safe for kids and pets.
Do you paint trim, doors, and baseboards as well?
Yes, we can repaint doors, window casings, crown molding, and baseboards using durable semi-gloss enamel to give your rooms a complete, fresh look.
Can you match my existing paint color?
Yes! If you have leftover paint or need us to color match an existing wall swatch, we work directly with paint suppliers to ensure an exact match.
